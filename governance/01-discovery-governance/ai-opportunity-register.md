## Purpose

ISO 42001 Clause 6.1 requires organizations to identify not only risks but also **opportunities** related to their AI management system. This register captures AI opportunities — business value, competitive advantage, innovation potential — so that governance is not purely defensive.

## Regulatory Basis

- **ISO/IEC 42001 Clause 6.1** — Actions to address risks and opportunities
- **NIST AI RMF GV-4.1** — Risk tolerance (opportunities inform risk appetite)
