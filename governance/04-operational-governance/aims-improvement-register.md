## Purpose

This register tracks continual improvement opportunities for the AIMS. ISO 42001 Clause 10.2 requires the organization to continually improve the suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the AIMS. Without a register, improvements are ad-hoc and untracked.

## Regulatory Basis

- **ISO/IEC 42001 Clause 10.2** — Continual improvement
- **NIST AI RMF GV-5.1** — Ongoing monitoring and review
