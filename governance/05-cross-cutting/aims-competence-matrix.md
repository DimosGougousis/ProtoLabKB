# AIMS Competence Matrix

> **Document Type:** Cross-Cutting — Workforce Competence
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Effective Date:** 2026-04-28
> **Owner:** Chief AI Officer (CAIO)
> **Approved By:** AI Governance Council
> **Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## Purpose

This matrix maps each governance role to the competences required to fulfill that role effectively. ISO 42001 Clause 7.2 requires organizations to determine the necessary competence of persons doing work under its control that affects AI system performance and safety.

## Regulatory Basis

- **ISO/IEC 42001 Clause 7.2** — Competence
- **NIST AI RMF GV-3.1** — Workforce AI literacy
- **EU AI Act Article 4** — AI literacy obligations
